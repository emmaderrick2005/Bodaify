# Bodaify — Screen Recording Script

**Title:** Bodaify - AI-powered fleet intelligence for Kampala boda boda operations
**Target Duration:** ~2 minutes

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & Dashboard (0:00 - 0:20)
* **Visual:** Open the Bodaify web application. The industrial dark theme is visible.
* **Action:** 
  1. Hover over the sidebar navigation elements to show the menu.
  2. Scroll down the Main Dashboard to highlight the top stat cards (Fleet Riders, Avg km/L Fleet, CBD Congestion, Avg Fleet Health).
  3. Briefly pause on the **Kampala Congestion Heatmap** and **Road Quality Index** charts.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "Welcome to Bodaify—the ultimate AI-powered fleet intelligence platform designed specifically for Kampala's boda boda operations. Let's explore the dashboard."

---

### Scene 2: Incline-Aware Routing & Mapbox (0:20 - 0:45)
* **Visual:** Click on the **Incline Routing (⛰)** tab from the sidebar.
* **Action:**
  1. Set **Origin** and **Destination**.
  2. Change **Route Mode** to `⛰ Incline-Aware`.
  3. Adjust **Rider Weight** (e.g., 75 kg) and **Load** (e.g., 20 kg).
  4. Click the **"🗺 Navigate"** button.
  5. Scroll down to show the **Mapbox Turn-by-Turn Navigation** map and the **Route Comparison** table comparing Eco, Fastest, Safest, and Incline-Aware modes.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "Our routing engine integrates Mapbox with incline-aware intelligence. By inputting the rider's weight and load, Bodaify calculates the most fuel-efficient route, avoiding steep Kampala hills."

---

### Scene 3: AI Jam Predictor (0:45 - 1:00)
* **Visual:** Click on the **Jam Predictor (◉)** tab.
* **Action:**
  1. Select an **Area** (e.g., Kampala CBD).
  2. Set the **Hour of Day** to a peak hour (e.g., `17` for 5 PM).
  3. Click **"Predict Congestion ◉"**.
  4. Show the prediction result and the **7-day weekly forecast chart**.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "Avoid traffic before it happens. The AI Jam Predictor models Kampala's traffic patterns, giving you area-specific congestion forecasts for any hour of the week."

---

### Scene 4: Road Quality & Fuel Optimization (1:00 - 1:25)
* **Visual:** Quick cuts between **Road Quality (⟁)** and **Fuel Optimizer (◬)** views.
* **Action:**
  1. **Road Quality:** Click **"Analyze Road ⟁"** to process accelerometer data and display the simulated Kampala road quality map.
  2. **Fuel Optimizer:** Switch views, adjust the **Distance** and **Avg Incline (%)**, then click **"Optimize ◬"**. Show the physics-based fuel-to-weight optimization result.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "We map road quality using accelerometer data to protect the bikes. Combine this with our physics-based Fuel Optimizer to calculate the exact fuel needed for any load and incline."

---

### Scene 5: Maintenance AI & DEM Elevation (1:25 - 1:45)
* **Visual:** Quick cuts between **Maintenance AI (⚙)** and **DEM Elevation (△)**.
* **Action:**
  1. **Maintenance AI:** Click **"Run Diagnostics ⚙"** on a mock bike's telemetry (Mileage, Brake Wear, Oil Level). Show the Fleet Health Overview.
  2. **DEM Elevation:** Switch views, click **"Generate Elevation Profile △"** to display the SRTM-style terrain chart between two coordinates.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "Prevent breakdowns with Predictive Maintenance AI analyzing real-time bike telemetry. Plus, visualize Kampala's terrain with Digital Elevation Models."

---

### Scene 6: Fuel Audit & Outro (1:45 - 2:00)
* **Visual:** Click on the **Fuel Audit (▦)** tab, then finish on the Dashboard.
* **Action:**
  1. Show the **Fuel Audit Dashboard** charts.
  2. Click **"Submit Entry ▦"** to log a fuel transaction.
  3. Click the **Theme Toggle (🌙/☀️)** in the top right to briefly show Light Mode, then switch back to Dark Mode.
  4. Open the User Avatar menu in the top right.
* **Voiceover/Text:** "Track every drop with the Fuel Audit dashboard. Bodaify—optimizing the boda boda industry for maximum efficiency. Try it today."
